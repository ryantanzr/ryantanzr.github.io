# Site redesign: The Bamboo Grove at Dawn

Mode: Experience on the home page (the artifact leads), Read on blog posts and resume. Audiences: recruiters and engineers, equal. Constraints: Lato kept, ink motifs kept, Asian aesthetic, subtle zen motion only, light and dark both, strict self-hosted CSP, CMS and CLI untouched, factual copy unchanged. Lotus, ensō, bodhi-leaf and mala-bead motifs only as seals, dividers, the theme toggle and the footer strand, never wallpaper. No Buddha figures, no wheel or swastika-type symbols.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a view into a bamboo grove at dawn: layered ink-wash stalks, drifting mist, a low gold sun. It refuses the developer-template hero (name, tagline, tech-icon grid) and the previous red-washi poster.

OWN-WORLD: Mist-green washi ground, bark-brown sumi, bamboo-green washes, dawn gold used for light, seals and rules, never text on light. Dark theme is the same grove before sunrise: forest-black ground, pale gold moon, firefly motes. Lato for text, Shippori Mincho for names and headings. Ink-wash filters on every drawn form; no boxes, no cards, hairline rules, a gold lotus seal.

STORY: A visitor sees a calm engineer's name in a grove, trusts the quiet, scrolls the path as a bamboo culm with one node per role, reads the work and the writing, and reaches the clearing (lotus pond) with the email link. Posts read on a calm, high-contrast column.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed grove: three depths of ink bamboo (pale far, mid, dark framing stalks at the edges), mist bands, gold sun low behind the stalks right of centre. Name in Shippori Mincho lower left over mist with the one-line pitch, a gold lotus ensō seal beside the location line, and two actions (Read the blog, Get in touch). The first section's heading is glimpsed through mist at the fold.

FORM: Assigned candidate chosen by the user from the decision page: "The Bamboo Grove at Dawn" (Impeccable's pick card, kind pick), seed:8235a25fc811bfbf. Signature interaction: slow living scene (stalks ease in over about two seconds, mist drifts, near stalks sway under one degree, scroll parallax where supported), the lotus toggle that opens or closes its petals while the page cross-fades, and gold light that lingers on hovered rows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
